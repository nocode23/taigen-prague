Head Mixologist · Taigen Prague

Founder & Head Mixologist

Tomas
Melzer

The creative force behind every drink

For Tomas, a cocktail is never just a drink. It is a carefully crafted experience — a meeting point between the guest, the season, and the land. Deeply influenced by Japanese aesthetics and the philosophy of the dedicated craftsman, he founded Taigen as a place where precision meets genuine warmth.

Every menu reflects months of sourcing, testing, and refining. Tomas works directly with Czech farmers and foragers to find ingredients at their absolute peak — then builds each cocktail from the ground up around that single ingredient.

The Craft

Precision in every step

Behind every cocktail is a process that begins long before the bar opens. Tomas sources, macerates, distils, and refines — testing combinations until the balance is exactly right. Nothing leaves the bar that hasn't been made with intention.

Farm to Glass

What we source

We source directly from Czech farms, foragers, and small producers. When a local ingredient is at its peak, it finds its way into our glasses. We build menus around what the season offers — not the other way around.

Guiding Principles

The Taigen Philosophy

01

Farm to Glass

We source directly from Czech farms, foragers, and small producers. When a local ingredient is at its peak, it finds its way into our glasses. We build menus around what the season offers — not the other way around.

02

Precision & Craft

Every drink at Taigen is approached with technical rigour and quiet dedication. We test, refine, and adjust until the balance is exactly right — because a cocktail that is almost perfect is not a Taigen cocktail.

03

Genuine Connection

Hospitality is the heart of any great bar. Taigen is built to feel like a home — warm, unhurried, and welcoming. We remember your preferences, celebrate your moments, and make you feel expected.
