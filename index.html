Our Philosophy
"Every drink tells a story of quality, sustainability, and genuine connection."

Taigen is a Japanese-inspired bar dedicated to craft cocktail mixology. Guided by a farm-to-glass philosophy, head mixologist Tomas Melzer carefully sources seasonal ingredients from local Czech farms and transforms them into unique cocktails that celebrate both nature and craftsmanship.
