The Menu

A menu that changes with the land and the season.

Every edition of Taigen's menu represents months of research, sourcing, and testing. Tomas works directly with farmers, foragers, and local producers to find ingredients at their absolute peak — then builds each cocktail from the ground up around that single ingredient.

Menu No. 6 celebrates the transition from summer into autumn: richer spirits, warmer spices, and the last fruits of the season.
